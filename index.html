About Ceptor Graphics

Ceptor Graphics is a design studio of one. I'm Ceptor, a student at Meru University of Science and Technology, and I build brands and websites alongside my studies.

I design the things a business shows the world: logos, posters, banners, business cards and invitation cards. I also build the websites that carry them.

Every project starts with a conversation about who you want to reach. Then I design until it looks like you.
